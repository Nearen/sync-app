Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

What the app does

Sync App is a personal data sync app used by its owner only. With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own data from his Google account and saves a copy on his own computer.

What happens to the data

  • The data is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Nothing is shared, sold or stored online.
  • The data is not used for advertising.
  • No one other than the owner can see the data.

Google API Limited Use

Sync App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Select Sync App, then choose "Delete all connections". Deleting the files on the computer removes the saved copy.
